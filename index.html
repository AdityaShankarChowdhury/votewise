How to Vote

Your step-by-step guide to exercising your democratic right.

✅
Step 1

Check Your Eligibility

Age 18+, citizenship, mental capacity, not disenfranchised.

Pro Tip: Check eligibility early as requirements vary by state/country.
📋
Step 2

Register to Vote

Gather documents needed, check deadlines, complete online vs offline registration.

Pro Tip: Keep your registration receipt safe!
📍
Step 3

Find Your Polling Booth

Using voter slip, official apps (like ECI app in India), or official websites.

Pro Tip: Verify your booth location a day before voting.
🧑‍💼
Step 4

Know Your Candidates

Research backgrounds, criminal records, and study party manifestos.

Pro Tip: Look for Form 26 (India) for detailed candidate info.
🗳️
Step 5

Cast Your Vote

Step inside booth, press EVM button or mark ballot, check VVPAT slip if applicable.

Pro Tip: Your vote is secret. No one can force you to reveal it.
📡
Step 6

Track Results

Follow official results websites and avoid early exit poll speculation.

Pro Tip: Official results are the only ones that matter!

Key Electoral Bodies

Election Commission of India

🇮🇳

Est. 1950

  • Conducts free & fair elections
  • Registers political parties
  • Enforces Model Code of Conduct
Official Website

Federal Election Commission

🇺🇸

Est. 1974

  • Administers campaign finance laws
  • Discloses campaign finance data
  • Enforces contribution limits
Official Website

Electoral Commission

🇬🇧

Est. 2001

  • Oversees elections and referendums
  • Regulates political finance
  • Registers political parties
